# Research workflow skills

1. Repository and evidence audit: inspect branch head, tasks, artifacts and data gates.
2. Universe discovery: extend historical market-cap ranks with free sources; distinguish candidate discovery from admissible past ranks.
3. Data engineering: normalize prices, cap, funding, instrument IDs and lifecycle data; preserve provenance and missingness.
4. Experimental design: preregister hypotheses, parameter axes, universe arms, endpoints, chronology and inference before outcomes.
5. Backtesting: use causal timing; separate price PnL, funding, fees and slippage.
6. Inference: preserve dependence, effective sample size, uncertainty and multiplicity controls.
7. Red-team review: test leakage, survivorship, ticker mapping, missing assets, delisting, costs and overfitting.
8. Reproducibility: stable experiment IDs/hashes, terminal cell statuses, idempotent resume and known-answer tests.
9. Recursive research: adapt future hypotheses to evidence, never rewrite past hypotheses or select a winner using sealed holdout outcomes.
